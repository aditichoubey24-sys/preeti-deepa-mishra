// about

Design that starts with people and ends with something real.

I bridge the gap between human behaviour and tangible execution. My work spans the whole product lifecycle: user research and ethnographic studies first, then UI/UX systems, brand identity and physical-digital prototypes.

Whether it's rebranding a legendary Indian cooperative, building trust between travellers and locals, or lighting up a festival campaign, I aim for work that is purposeful, polished and rooted in what people actually need.
